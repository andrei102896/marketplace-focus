Quick answers: what Marketplace Focus is, who it is for, and what it solves

Direct answers for people who still need Facebook but do not want Reels loops.

What is Marketplace Focus?

Marketplace Focus is a privacy-first browser extension that blocks Facebook Reels.

It keeps Facebook usable for Marketplace, Groups, Messenger, friends, family, and regular browsing. See the setup walkthrough for a quick start.

Who is it for?

It is for people who still need Facebook but want to avoid endless Reels scrolling.

Marketplace buyers, sellers, flippers, and researchers are a key use case, but the blocker also helps with Groups, Messenger, friends, family, and everyday Facebook browsing.

What problem does it solve?

It reduces the pull of short-video loops without turning Facebook into an all-or-nothing block.

Strict mode can redirect Reels pages, while Classic Facebook Mode focuses on hiding Reels entry points.

FAQ: blocking Facebook Reels while still using Facebook

Concise answers first, then supporting detail.

What is Marketplace Focus?

Short answer: It is a privacy-first browser extension that blocks Facebook Reels.

It helps keep Facebook useful for Marketplace, Groups, Messenger, friends, family, and regular browsing instead of short-video detours.

Who is Marketplace Focus for?

Short answer: It is for people who still need Facebook but want less endless scrolling.

Marketplace buyers, sellers, flippers, and researchers are important users, but not the only users.

How does Marketplace Focus block Facebook Reels?

Short answer: It can hide Reels entry points and, in strict mode, redirect Reels pages.

You can choose Strict Focus Mode or Classic Facebook Mode depending on how aggressively you want to block Reels.

Can I temporarily disable blocking?

Short answer: Yes, you can pause or disable it from the popup.

You can also require confirmation and enable Hard Lock cooldowns to avoid impulsive toggling.

Will a schedule override my manual toggle?

Short answer: Yes, when schedule is enabled, active status follows your selected time windows.

This keeps protection consistent across recurring browsing sessions.

Can I allow Reels on specific pages?

Short answer: Yes, you can create exceptions for specific pages, paths, or URL patterns.

Exceptions let you keep global protection while allowing specific workflows when needed.

Does Marketplace Focus work on Chrome, Edge, and Firefox?

Short answer: Yes, it supports Chrome and also has Edge and Firefox builds.

The core focus controls are built to stay consistent across supported browsers.

Is Marketplace Focus free and privacy-first?

Short answer: Yes. It has no account, no tracking, and local browser settings.

You can review storage and data-collection details at /privacy before installing.

Where do I get help or report issues?

Short answer: Use the Marketplace Focus support page.

The support page links the store support path and lists the details to include for faster troubleshooting.

Is Marketplace Focus affiliated with Meta or Facebook?

Short answer: No. Marketplace Focus is independent and is not affiliated with Meta or Facebook.

It is a browser extension that runs locally in your browser to block Facebook Reels surfaces.
