Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 18, 2026

Data Collection

Marketplace Focus does not require account creation, does not track you, does not sell personal data, and does not send browsing history to a remote service.

Settings Storage

The extension stores your settings locally in your browser (blocking mode, pause duration, exceptions, hard lock, and schedule preferences).

Network Requests

The extension does not use analytics, remote logging, tracking pixels, or external scripts to enforce blocking behavior on facebook.com pages.

Policy Updates

This page may be updated when product behavior changes. Material changes should be reflected with a revised effective date.

Contact

For support, use the extension listing support channel on the Chrome Web Store.

Privacy FAQ

Does Marketplace Focus collect account-level personal data?

Short answer: No, it does not require account creation for use.

The extension is built to run with local settings stored in your browser.

Does Marketplace Focus use analytics or tracking pixels?

Short answer: No. The extension does not use analytics, remote logging, tracking pixels, or external scripts.

Blocking behavior runs in your browser against Reels surfaces on facebook.com.

Where can I get support if I have a privacy question?

Short answer: Use the Chrome Web Store support path listed on the extension listing.

You can also use the browser support page for compatibility context.
