Use Facebook Marketplace without Reels distractions

Short answer: Marketplace Focus helps buyers, sellers, flippers, and researchers keep Marketplace sessions centered on listings instead of Reels.

Marketplace requires focus

Marketplace browsing is often practical work: compare prices, read seller details, check condition, message quickly, and decide whether a listing is worth pursuing.

That workflow benefits from fewer short-video interruptions.

Reels interrupt buying, selling, and research workflows

Buyers

Stay with listings, seller profiles, saved searches, and messages instead of drifting into Reels.

Sellers

Keep posting, checking responses, and managing listings without a short-video detour.

Flippers and researchers

Protect sourcing windows and listing research when timing and attention matter.

Marketplace Focus Mode

Marketplace Focus Mode is the product's original wedge: use Facebook Marketplace with fewer Reels distractions. It works alongside Classic Facebook Mode and Strict Focus Mode, depending on how much blocking you want.

The extension remains narrow: it targets Facebook Reels rather than becoming a generic website blocker.

Hard Lock and schedules

If Marketplace sessions are recurring or high-value, Hard Lock can add friction before disabling blocking, and scheduled focus windows can turn blocking on during selected days and hours.

These controls are optional. Start simple, then add stronger controls only if Reels keeps interrupting your workflow.

Privacy and independence

Marketplace Focus stores settings locally in your browser, requires no account, and does not use tracking, analytics, remote logging, or data selling.

Marketplace Focus is independent and is not affiliated with Meta or Facebook.
