Block Facebook Reels

Short answer: Marketplace Focus helps you block Facebook Reels while keeping useful parts of Facebook available, including Marketplace, Groups, Messenger, friends, and family.

Why block Facebook Reels?

Many Facebook sessions have a specific purpose: check Marketplace, reply in Messenger, catch up with Groups, or see updates from friends and family. Reels can turn those short sessions into endless scrolling.

Marketplace Focus is built for people who still need Facebook, but want Reels out of the way.

What Marketplace Focus blocks

Reels pages

Strict Focus Mode can redirect Facebook Reels pages so you are not left inside the Reels surface.

Reels entry points

The extension hides or reduces common Reels buttons, links, and modules where Facebook exposes them.

Bypass moments

Optional confirmations, Hard Lock, and scheduled windows add friction when you want stronger protection.

What still works

Marketplace Focus is not an all-Facebook blocker. Marketplace, Groups, Messenger, friends, family, and regular browsing can remain available while Reels is blocked or reduced.

You can also add exceptions for specific pages or paths when a workflow needs them.

Privacy-first by design

No account is required. Marketplace Focus stores settings locally in your browser and does not use tracking, analytics, remote logging, or data selling.

Marketplace Focus is independent and is not affiliated with Meta or Facebook.
