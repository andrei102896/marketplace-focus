Hard Lock and scheduled focus for Facebook Reels blocking

Short answer: Hard Lock adds friction before disable actions, and schedules automate protection so blocking stays active when you need it most.

What Hard Lock is and when to use it

Hard Lock introduces a cooldown delay before blocking can be turned off. Use it if you often disable protection impulsively during Facebook sessions.

It is most effective when your distraction risk is high and you need a pause before making a disable decision.

How scheduled focus helps recurring workflows

Schedules let you set blocking by day and time. This is useful for repeat buying windows, nightly inventory scans, fixed research sessions, or any recurring Facebook use that should stay Reels-free.

When schedule is active, your protection state follows those windows automatically.
