Use Facebook without Reels

Short answer: Marketplace Focus blocks Facebook Reels so Facebook can stay useful for Marketplace, Groups, Messenger, friends, family, and regular browsing.

Facebook can still be useful

People still use Facebook for practical reasons: local buying and selling, community Groups, direct messages, events, and family updates. The problem is not always Facebook itself. Often, it is getting pulled from a useful task into Reels.

Reels are the part many people want to avoid

Reels are designed to keep attention moving. Marketplace Focus targets that specific surface instead of positioning itself as a generic productivity blocker.

The goal is narrower: keep Facebook available, but make Reels harder to fall into.

Keep the useful parts of Facebook

Marketplace

Browse listings, message sellers, and compare options with fewer Reels interruptions.

Groups and Messenger

Check communities and conversations without turning the session into short-video scrolling.

Friends and family

Keep casual Facebook browsing available while reducing the pull of Reels entry points.

Choose Classic Facebook Mode

Classic Facebook Mode is the lighter option. It focuses on hiding Reels entry points while keeping the rest of Facebook available. If Reels still pulls you in, Strict Focus Mode adds redirect protection.
